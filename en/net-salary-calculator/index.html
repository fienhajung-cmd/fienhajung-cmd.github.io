Take-home pay, PPh 21 withholding, BPJS contributions, and total employer cost. Everything runs in your browser.

How it works

Since 2024, employers withhold monthly PPh 21 with an average effective rate (TER) table from January to November. In December they recompute the full-year tax at Article 17 progressive rates and withhold the difference.

Example: a base salary of IDR 10,000,000 with status TK/0 and 0.24% JKK. Employer-paid JKK, JKM, and BPJS Health premiums bring taxable gross to IDR 10,454,000, which falls in the 2.5% band of category A, so monthly PPh 21 is IDR 261,350. After employee contributions of IDR 400,000, take-home pay is IDR 9,338,650.

What this does not cover

It assumes the same pay for a full calendar year. It leaves out bonuses, THR, overtime, part-year employment, extra pension schemes, government tax incentives, and special situations such as non-resident tax treatment or treaty relief. Some foreign workers have different BPJS rules, so confirm your own case.

This is an estimate, not tax advice. Check the official rules or a licensed tax consultant before relying on it for payroll.
